# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss technical decisions with evidence, give useful
feedback, and allow people to correct mistakes. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, and
publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation to protect the project
community. Contact a maintainer privately through their published contact information to report a
conduct concern. Respect the privacy of everyone involved and avoid retaliation.

A dedicated reporting contact and enforcement process must be established before the public release.
